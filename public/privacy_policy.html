Privacy Policy

BookLife is a free, open-source, unofficial Android client for bookmeter.com. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by bookmeter. This page explains what information the app handles. If you use the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Information Collection and Use

The app has no server of its own. It talks directly to bookmeter.com on your behalf:

  • Login credentials. The email address and password you enter on the sign-in screen are sent only to bookmeter.com over HTTPS to sign you in. They are not stored on your device and are never sent anywhere else.
  • Session cookie. After you sign in, bookmeter.com returns a session cookie. The app keeps this cookie (and your bookmeter user ID) locally on your device so that you stay signed in. They are deleted when you sign out from Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Your reading data (bookshelves, reviews, search queries) is exchanged only between the app and bookmeter.com. The developer of this app does not receive it.
  • Camera. Barcode scanning uses Google's code scanner (Google Play services). The app itself does not request the camera permission and does not store images.

Accounts

The app does not create accounts. Accounts are created, managed and deleted on bookmeter.com; to delete your account or data, please use bookmeter's own account settings and contact bookmeter. Signing out of the app removes the local session data.

Third-party Services

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect information used to identify you or your device:

  • Firebase Crashlytics – collects crash reports and diagnostic logs (e.g. device model, OS version, stack traces, app state) to help fix bugs.
  • Firebase Analytics – collects usage events (e.g. which features are used) and an app-instance identifier. The app does not show ads and does not use the advertising ID.

Log Data

When an error occurs in the app, the above services may collect Log Data such as your device's IP address, device name, operating system version, the app configuration, the time and date of use, and other statistics.

Security

All communication with bookmeter.com uses HTTPS. We strive to protect your information, but no method of transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Links to Other Sites

The app may open links (such as bookmeter.com pages or Amazon) in your browser. These sites are not operated by the developer, who has no control over and assumes no responsibility for their content or privacy practices.

This app is not directed to anyone under the age of 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us through the link below.
